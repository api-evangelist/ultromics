# Ultromics

> Ultromics is a UK-based clinical AI company (GV-backed, Oxford spin-out) whose EchoGo platform applies FDA 510(k)-cleared AI to routine echocardiograms to detect heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) and cardiac amyloidosis. EchoGo integrates with existing hospital echo workflows and PACS environments; Ultromics publishes no public developer API.

## Products

- [EchoGo Platform](https://www.ultromics.com/about-ultromics-the-platform): Cloud AI analysis of echocardiograms, delivered through clinical systems and PACS
- [Clinical Evidence](https://www.ultromics.com/clinical-evidence): Published studies backing EchoGo Heart Failure and EchoGo Amyloidosis
