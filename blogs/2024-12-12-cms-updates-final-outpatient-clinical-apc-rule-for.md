Ultromics announced today that CMS has released the final OPPS rules in the Federal Register. As anticipated, CMS has assigned the new Category III CPT code 0932T to APC 5743, with a reimbursement rate of $299.91. The assignment to a clinical APC highlights Medicare’s confidence in the robust clinical validation of Ultromics’ technology, underscoring the value of the data and its impact on heart failure care.
