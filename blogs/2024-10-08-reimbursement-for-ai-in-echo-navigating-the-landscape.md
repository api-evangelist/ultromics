Dr Ross Upton PhD, Founder, CEO and Chief Scientific Officer, Ultromics, and AI Co-Chair, HeartShare, NIH Innovative technologies such as AI-driven echocardiography diagnostics (AI Echo Dx) are transforming the healthcare landscape, enhancing patient care, and streamlining clinical pathways. For developers of new technology, however, the practicalities of moving their innovations into clinical use can be monumental due to the challenges of navigating the complexities of medical device reimbursement.
