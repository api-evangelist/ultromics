Cardiac amyloidosis (CA) is a progressive, underrecognized disease caused by the deposition of misfolded proteins in the heart and other organs. Despite growing clinical awareness and the availability of disease-modifying therapies, diagnosis is often missed or delayed. 1 CA is a known but frequently missed cause of heart failure, particularly in patients with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF).
