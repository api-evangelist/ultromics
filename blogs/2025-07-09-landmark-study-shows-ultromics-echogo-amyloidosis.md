Clinical Publication: Cardiac Amyloidosis Detection From a Single Echocardiographic Video Clip: A Novel Artificial Intelligence-Based Screening Tool See paper ➢ Cardiac amyloidosis is an often-missed, life-threatening cause of heart failure. As new disease-modifying therapies emerge, timely and earlier diagnosis is critical. EchoGo® Amyloidosis is the first FDA-cleared AI screening tool for cardiac amyloidosis using an echocardiogram and has received Breakthrough Device designation from the FDA.
