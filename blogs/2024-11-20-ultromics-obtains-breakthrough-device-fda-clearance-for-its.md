EchoGo® Amyloidosis is an AI-based software-only medical device, which aims to improve early detection of Cardiac Amyloidosis, an underdiagnosed cause of heart failure. EchoGo® Amyloidosis sits on Ultromics' EchoGo® platform complementing the existing commercialized EchoGo® Heart Failure device.
